About Safe Steer AI

Safe Steer is an autonomous, on-device edge AI driver safety system engineered to eliminate fatigue-induced collisions, distracted driving, and unresponsive vehicular emergencies in real time.

High-Precision Eye & Face Mesh

Using Google MediaPipe's neural 3D FaceLandmarker, Safe Steer tracks 468 spatial coordinates in the driver's eye sockets and calculates the Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) at up to 60 FPS, distinguishing between natural blinks and dangerous microsleep episodes.

Spatial Vector Distraction Radar

By computing Euclidean distance vectors between the driver's nose landmark and hand landmarks (thumb tip #4 and index tip #8), the vision engine instantly differentiates normal steering wheel maneuvers from hazardous phone usage.

Graduated Acoustic Deterrence

When driver fatigue is detected, the engine emits graduated auditory alerts: conversational synthesized speech reminders, high-frequency alert beeps, and an escalated multi-second car horn siren if the driver remains unresponsive.

Forensic Incident Telemetry

All safety breaches are committed to an immutable SQLite audit log with driver IDs, vehicle tags, and microsecond-level timestamps, providing transport managers with actionable telematics.
